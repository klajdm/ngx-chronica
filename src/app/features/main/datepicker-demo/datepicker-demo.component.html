Datepicker Demo

Popup mode datepicker with a customizable trigger and form integration.

Example

<chronica-datepicker
			[selectedDate]="selectedDate"
			[config]="{ theme: 'light', colorTheme: 'blue' }"
			(dateSelected)="onDateSelected($event)"
		>
			<button class="px-3 py-2 border rounded">Pick a date</button>
		</chronica-datepicker>

Use `dateSelected` output to receive the chosen date and integrate with forms or state.
